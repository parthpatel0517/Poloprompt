How to Write a Good AI Image Prompt

          
Most disappointing AI images come from short prompts. "A dog in a field" leaves the model to guess the breed, the style, the light, the camera angle and the mood, and it fills every gap with the most average choice. A strong image prompt answers five questions: what is in the picture, what style it is in, how it is lit, how it is framed, and what to leave out. This generator walks you through each one and formats the result for the tool you use.

          
Want inspiration first? The Image Library has ready-made prompts for trending styles like claymation, pixel art and action figures, and our guide to getting AI to render text correctly helps with signs, posters and logos.

        


        

          
AI Image Prompt Generator FAQ

          

            Which AI image tools does this work with?
            
It formats the prompt for ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Midjourney, and Stable Diffusion or Flux. ChatGPT and Gemini get clear, plain-language instructions; Midjourney gets a single line with parameters like --ar and --no; Stable Diffusion and Flux get separate positive and negative prompts plus a recommended image size.

          

          

            Can I use it to restyle my own photo?
            
Yes. Tick "I'm restyling my own photo" and the prompt tells the AI to keep the subject's face, features and pose recognizable while changing only the style, lighting and setting. Upload or attach your photo in the AI tool, then paste the prompt.

          

          

            How do I get text to appear correctly in the image?
            
Type the exact words in the "Text in the image" field. The prompt puts them in quotes and asks for them to be spelled letter for letter. Keep text short, a few words at most, and regenerate if a letter comes out wrong, since AI image tools can still misspell.

          

          

            Why does my result look different from what I described?
            
Image models fill in anything you leave out with their own guesses. The more specific your subject is, such as who or what, doing what, and where, the closer the result. Change one option at a time between attempts so you can see what each choice does.

          

          

            What does the Surprise Me button do?
            
It picks a random subject, style, lighting, shot and aspect ratio and builds a prompt instantly. It is a quick way to explore styles or get unstuck when you don't know what to make.

          

          

            Is this AI image prompt generator free, and is my data stored?
            
Yes, it is completely free with no sign-up. The generator runs entirely in your browser, so nothing you type is sent to a server or stored. PoloPrompt doesn't generate the images itself; you paste the prompt into the AI tool you already use.
