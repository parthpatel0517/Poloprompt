FAQ: E-commerce Product Description Prompts

          

            Why do I need a different prompt for each marketplace?
            
Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, and Meesho each have different algorithms, character limits, and shopper expectations. Amazon rewards keyword-dense titles and exactly five scannable bullet points because of how its search and mobile app render listings. Shopify stores are self-hosted, so the priority shifts to on-page SEO and a persuasive brand-voice description. Etsy buyers respond to handmade, story-driven language and expect materials/dimensions up front. Meesho's audience is highly price-sensitive and browses mostly on mobile, so short, plain-language bullets convert better than elaborate copy. This tool bakes those differences directly into the generated prompt.

          

          

            What should I put in the "Product Name & Core Material/Feature" field?
            
Be as specific as you can — brand name, product type, and the one material or feature that makes it distinct (e.g. "stainless steel," "handwoven cotton," "wireless"). The more concrete detail you give the AI, the less generic the finished title and bullet points will be. You can always edit the output afterward to add sizing, color variants, or a promo detail.

          

          

            Can I select more than one benefit?
            
Yes — check as many benefits as genuinely apply to your product. The generated prompt instructs the AI to weave all of them naturally into the bullets and description rather than just listing the words, so selecting two or three well-matched benefits usually produces a stronger, more specific listing than selecting all four.

          

          

            Will the output be ready to paste straight into my listing?
            
The AI's response will be close to publish-ready, but you should always double-check factual claims (materials, dimensions, certifications) before publishing, since the AI doesn't know your product beyond what you typed in. For Amazon specifically, also verify the title length and backend search terms comply with the current Amazon Seller Central character limits, which do change occasionally.

          

          

            How is this better than asking ChatGPT directly for a product description?
            
A bare "write a product description for X" prompt gives the AI almost no constraints, so it defaults to generic filler like "unlock the perfect addition to your home" — the same phrasing thousands of other sellers get too. This generator instead assembles a marketplace-specific format spec (Amazon's 5-bullet structure, Etsy's story-driven tone, Meesho's price-sensitive plain language), a worked example matched to your chosen marketplace, and a hard ban on overused listing clichés. The result reads like it was written by someone who sells on that platform every day, not a generic AI answer — and you get 2-3 angle options in one pass instead of manually re-rolling.

          

          

            Is this tool free, and is my product information stored anywhere?
            
Yes, completely free with no sign-up. Everything runs client-side in your browser using JavaScript — your product name, benefits, and marketplace selection are never sent to a server or saved anywhere. You can generate as many listing prompts as you need.
