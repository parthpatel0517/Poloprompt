FAQ: Planning AI Automations

          

            What tools do these automation ideas rely on?
            
Each idea references realistic, widely-used tools — Zapier and Make.com for connecting apps, Airtable and Google Sheets for lightweight databases, Twilio for SMS/WhatsApp, and platform-specific tools like Shopify, QuickBooks, HubSpot, Calendly, Mailchimp, and Buffer depending on the use case. You don't need every tool listed — most ideas can be rebuilt with whatever automation platform and CRM you already use; the pattern (trigger → action) is what matters.

          

          

            Do I need to know how to code to build these?
            
No. Tools like Zapier and Make.com are no-code/low-code platforms designed for exactly this kind of trigger-and-action automation. The "Implementation Prompt" column is written so you can hand it to an AI assistant (or a freelance automation builder) and get clear, actionable build instructions without writing code yourself.

          

          

            How do I choose which automation idea to build first?
            
Start with whichever idea removes the most repetitive manual work or fixes the most common bottleneck in your business right now — usually the one your team complains about most. Automations that touch customer-facing response time (like missed-call text-back or instant FAQ bots) tend to have the fastest, most visible payoff, which also makes them easier to justify building first.

          

          

            Can I combine multiple ideas into one bigger workflow?
            
Yes, and it's often the best approach. Many of these ideas are designed to chain together — for example, a lead capture automation can feed directly into a nurture sequence automation. Once you're comfortable with one automation, use the Zapier / Make Workflow Planner tool on this site to map out a multi-step workflow that links several ideas into one continuous pipeline.

          

          

            Is this tool free, and are my selections stored anywhere?
            
Yes, completely free with no sign-up. The industry and goal you select are processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript to look up matching ideas — nothing is sent to a server or saved. Try different industry/goal combinations as many times as you like.
