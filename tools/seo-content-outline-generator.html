FAQ: SEO Content Outline Prompts

          

            What's the difference between this and just asking an AI to "write a blog post"?
            
Asking an AI to write directly from a bare topic usually produces generic, unfocused content with weak on-page SEO — and it tends to lean on the same filler phrases every AI writer defaults to ("in today's digital age," "let's dive in"). This tool first builds a structured outline — headings, word count targets, meta title/description options, and search-intent alignment — with a worked example locking in the expected specificity, and an explicit ban on those overused AI phrases baked into the prompt itself. Planning the structure before the prose, with those guardrails in place, consistently produces better-organized, more search-friendly, and less obviously-AI-sounding content than a bare "write a blog post" request.

          

          

            Why does search intent matter for the outline?
            
Google ranks pages that match what the searcher actually wants to do. A reader searching an informational query wants clear explanations, while a transactional searcher wants pricing and a clear next step. Building the wrong intent into your outline is one of the most common reasons pages struggle to rank even when the keyword targeting is correct, so this tool bakes your selected intent directly into the structural guidance.

          

          

            Should I add secondary keywords even if I'm not sure which ones to use?
            
You don't have to — if you leave the field blank, the generated prompt instructs the AI to suggest 3-5 relevant secondary keywords itself and weave them into the outline. If you already have keyword research from a tool like Ahrefs, Semrush, or Google Keyword Planner, pasting those terms in directly will produce a more precisely targeted outline.

          

          

            How accurate are the word count targets per section?
            
They're a helpful planning guide, not a hard rule. The AI distributes your total target word count across the outline's sections proportionally to their importance, which gives you a realistic sense of how much content each H2 needs — useful for briefing a writer or estimating how long the finished piece will take to draft.

          

          

            Is this tool free, and is my keyword data stored anywhere?
            
Yes, it's completely free with no account required. The entire outline prompt is assembled in your browser with JavaScript — your keyword, secondary keywords, and selections are never transmitted to a server or saved. Use it as many times as you need for different articles.
