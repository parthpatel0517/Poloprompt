Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PoloPrompt ("we," "us," "our," or "the site") provides free, client-side AI prompt generators and workflow planning tools. This Privacy Policy explains what information is and isn't collected when you use poloprompt.com, and how the small amount of data we do encounter is handled.

No Account or Login Required

PoloPrompt does not require you to create an account, register, or provide an email address to use any tool on this site. There is no login system, no user profile, and no password to manage. You can use every generator anonymously, as many times as you like.

No Server-Side Storage of Tool Inputs

All of our prompt generators, the automation idea finder, and the workflow step planner run entirely as client-side JavaScript inside your own browser. When you type a product name, a keyword, a caption topic, or any other input into a form on this site, that text is processed locally on your device to build the output you see on screen. It is never transmitted to, logged by, or stored on any PoloPrompt server. Refreshing or closing the page clears whatever you typed, since nothing was saved anywhere in the first place.

What Information Is Collected

While we do not collect the content you type into our tools, some limited technical information may be collected in the ordinary course of operating a public website:

  • Advertising cookies: PoloPrompt may display ads served through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites, and to measure ad performance. You can learn more about how Google uses this data and manage your ad personalization settings at Google's Ads Policy page.
  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregated site usage, such as page views, traffic source, device/browser information, and interactions with site pages. Google may use cookies or similar technologies to provide this service. We do not send the text you enter into our prompt tools to Google Analytics.
  • Server logs: Like most web hosts, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps for security and infrastructure purposes. This is standard web server behavior and is not used to identify individual visitors for marketing purposes.

Cookie Policy

PoloPrompt itself does not set cookies to track you across sessions or store your tool inputs. Any cookies present on this site are most likely set by third-party services embedded on the page, primarily Google AdSense for ad personalization and measurement, as described above. Your browser settings allow you to block or delete cookies at any time; doing so may result in less relevant ads but will not affect your ability to use any PoloPrompt tool.

Third-Party Advertising Disclosure

This site may use Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Your Rights

Because PoloPrompt does not collect or store personal data or tool input content on its own servers, there is generally no personal data of yours held by us to access, correct, or delete. If you have concerns about data collected by third-party services embedded on this site (such as Google AdSense), you can manage your preferences directly through those services using the links above, or through your browser's cookie and privacy settings. Depending on your jurisdiction (for example, under GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights regarding data collected by third-party advertising partners — please refer to Google's own privacy documentation for details specific to their services.

Children's Privacy

PoloPrompt is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Our tools are intended for marketers, creators, and business owners.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how PoloPrompt operates, you can reach us at support@poloprompt.com. For general help using a tool, see help@poloprompt.com or our Contact page.
