About PoloPrompt

PoloPrompt started from a simple frustration: writing a good AI prompt for a repetitive marketing task — an ad, a product listing, a caption, a content outline — takes longer than it should, and most people end up rewriting the same structure from scratch every single time. We built PoloPrompt to remove that friction. Instead of staring at a blank chat window trying to remember the right framework or platform formatting rules, you fill in a few fields and get a complete, structured prompt ready to paste into ChatGPT, Claude, or any AI assistant you use.

Every tool on this site is free and always will be. There's no account to create, no email to hand over, and no usage cap. We believe the best utilities are the ones that get out of your way — you land on the page, use the tool, copy your result, and move on with your day. That philosophy shapes every decision on this site: no login walls, no forced newsletter sign-ups, no artificial limits pushing you toward a paid tier.

PoloPrompt is built for marketers running ad campaigns across Meta and Google, e-commerce sellers listing products on Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, or Meesho, content creators writing captions for Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube, SEO writers planning article structure, and small business owners exploring what automation tools like Zapier and Make.com can do for their workflow. Whether you're a solo freelancer or part of a marketing team, the goal is the same: fewer minutes spent on repetitive setup, more time spent on the work that actually needs a human's judgment.

Every generator runs entirely as client-side JavaScript in your browser — nothing you type is sent to a server or stored anywhere, by us or anyone else. We plan to keep adding new tools as we find more repetitive prompt-writing tasks worth automating. If there's a tool you'd like to see, our door is always open — see our Contact page for how to reach us.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do the tools actually work?

Each tool is a plain HTML page with a small piece of JavaScript that runs directly in your browser. When you fill in the form and click generate, the script assembles a text prompt (or, for the automation and workflow tools, a table or plan) from your inputs and a set of built-in formatting rules. There's no server processing your request — the entire thing happens on your device.

Is any of my data stored or sent anywhere?

No. None of the text you type into any PoloPrompt tool is transmitted to a server, logged, or saved. Once you close or refresh the page, your inputs are gone unless you copied the output yourself. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Do I need an OpenAI, Anthropic, or other AI account to use these tools?

Not to use PoloPrompt itself — generating a prompt here is completely free and independent of any AI provider. You'll need access to an AI assistant like ChatGPT or Claude separately to paste the generated prompt into and get a finished result, but PoloPrompt itself doesn't require any AI account or API key.
